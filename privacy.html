Privacy Policy

How IMM Food Innovators LLP handles information you share with us through this website.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by IMM Food Innovators LLP, Nhavi Hambardi Road, Nhavi, Yawal, Jalgaon, Maharashtra – 425524, India (“IMM”, “we”, “us”). We manufacture dehydrated fruit & vegetable powders, spices and herbal powders for business customers. You can reach us at info@immfoodinnovators.in or +91 9114403000.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us through our inquiry and quote forms, by email, or by WhatsApp:

  • Name, company name, business email address and phone / WhatsApp number
  • The product(s) you are interested in, estimated order volume, delivery destination or port, and your message

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also record technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in standard server logs used for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare quotations, samples and specifications
  • To manage orders, shipping and customer communication
  • To keep our website secure and to prevent spam or misuse

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers needed to run the website and answer you:

  • Web3Forms – delivers the contents of our forms to our email inbox
  • WhatsApp (Meta) – if you choose to message us on WhatsApp
  • Google – the embedded map (Google Maps) and web fonts (Google Fonts) are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address when those load
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) – serves our icon font
  • Hostinger – hosts this website

We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our own pages do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content, such as Google Maps, may set its own cookies according to its own policies. If we add website analytics in future, we will update this policy before doing so.

6. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and correspondence records for as long as needed to respond to you, to carry out any resulting business relationship, and to meet legal and accounting requirements. After that we delete or anonymise them.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@immfoodinnovators.in. We handle personal information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have additional rights under local data-protection law, and you can contact us to exercise them.

8. Security

This website is served over HTTPS and our forms include spam protection. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information through our forms.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date below shows when it last changed.
